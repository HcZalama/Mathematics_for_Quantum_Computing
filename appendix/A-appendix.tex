\paragraph{Conventions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The inner product is conjugate-linear in its \emph{first} argument. (Many mathematics texts conjugate the second.)
  \item Matrices of operators are taken in orthonormal bases unless stated otherwise; ``transpose and conjugate'' is the adjoint only there.
  \item The columns of the matrix of a linear map are the images of the basis vectors.
  \item For two qubits the basis is ordered $\ket{00},\ket{01},\ket{10},\ket{11}$, and the matrix of $A\tp B$ is the Kronecker product with $B$ varying fastest.
  \item Unless a value is given, $\hbar$ may be set to $1$.
  \item Logarithms in information-theoretic formulas are in base $2$ (entropy in bits), unless written $\ln$; $0\log0=0$.
  \item The Fourier transform on $\R$ is normalised to be unitary, with $e^{-\ii kx}$ in the forward transform.
\end{itemize}
